Same scale and framing, plain background. The photos become the anchor frames, so the 360 passes through exactly these two images and loops.

What happens

1. Both photos are padded to the generation canvas (no crop) in their own background colour.
2. Half 1: image-to-video from the front photo to the rear photo. Half 2: rear photo back to the front photo. Seams are pixel-exact.
3. Each half is checked for a mid-turn “teleport”. If found, the clean part is kept and the rest is regenerated from its last clean frame.
4. Pieces are joined on their shared frames, time-remapped to constant speed (the model eases at anchors), and interpolated with RIFE to the output fps.

Cost at 768p on the 4080: about 9 min per half plus 5 min per repair, then under a minute of post. The 24 fps pieces are kept next to the result.

Then key the background out downstream: the backdrop is flat, there is no floor contact shadow.
